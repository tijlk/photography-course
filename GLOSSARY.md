# Photography Glossary

Canonical terms for this course. A term is added once the learner has shown they can use it correctly. Lessons, reference sheets and learning records use these words.

## Exposure

**Stop**:
A doubling or halving of the light that makes up an exposure, however it is changed (aperture, shutter speed or ISO).
_Avoid_: step, EV step (except when reading camera menus, which say EV)

**Reciprocity**:
Trading one setting against another stop for stop so the exposure stays the same, e.g. one stop wider aperture with one stop faster shutter.
_Avoid_: equivalent exposure, exposure trade

**ISO**:
The camera's sensitivity setting. Each doubling needs one stop less light, at the cost of more noise.
_Avoid_: ASA, film speed, gain

**Histogram**:
A graph of the image's tones from dark (left) to bright (right). Data piled against an edge means detail clipped to pure black or pure white.
_Avoid_: levels graph

## Focus and sharpness

**Depth of field**:
The distance between the nearest and farthest points that look acceptably sharp. It increases with a higher f-number, greater subject distance or a shorter focal length.
_Avoid_: DoF (fine in notes, spell out in lessons), focus range

## Files

**RAW**:
A file holding the sensor data with the camera's settings recorded but not applied, so white balance and tone can be redone in editing.
_Avoid_: digital negative, raw format (lower-case is fine in running text)
