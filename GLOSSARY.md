# Photography Glossary

Canonical terms for this course. A term is added once the learner has shown they can use it correctly. Lessons, reference sheets and learning records use these words.

## Exposure

**Stop**:
A doubling or halving of the light that makes up an exposure, however it is changed (aperture, shutter speed or ISO).
_Avoid_: step, EV step (except when reading camera menus, which say EV)

**Reciprocity**:
Trading one setting against another stop for stop so the exposure stays the same, e.g. one stop wider aperture with one stop faster shutter.
_Avoid_: equivalent exposure, exposure trade

**ISO**:
The camera's sensitivity setting. Each doubling needs one stop less light, at the cost of more noise.
_Avoid_: ASA, film speed, gain

**Histogram**:
A graph of the image's tones from dark (left) to bright (right). Data piled against an edge means detail clipped to pure black or pure white.
_Avoid_: levels graph

**Exposure compensation**:
A deliberate offset, in stops, from the meter's suggested exposure. Plus for scenes brighter than middle grey (snow), minus for darker ones.
_Avoid_: EV shift, exposure bias

**Exposure simulation**:
The viewfinder setting that makes the EVF image follow your exposure settings. With it off, the view stays at standard brightness.
_Avoid_: live exposure preview (fine as a description, but use the one term)

**White balance**:
The camera setting, in kelvin, that removes the colour cast of the light. Light lower in kelvin than the setting looks warmer (orange); light higher in kelvin looks cooler (blue).
_Avoid_: colour correction, WB (fine in notes)

## Focus and sharpness

**Depth of field**:
The distance between the nearest and farthest points that look acceptably sharp. It increases with a higher f-number, greater subject distance or a shorter focal length.
_Avoid_: DoF (fine in notes, spell out in lessons), focus range

**35mm-equivalent focal length**:
Focal length × crop factor: the full-frame focal length that gives the same field of view. The handheld rule uses this number.
_Avoid_: effective focal length, "crop focal length"

**In-body image stabilisation (IBIS)**:
Moving the sensor to counter camera shake. It's rated in stops of slower handheld shutter speed and does nothing for a moving subject.
_Avoid_: IS (reserve for lens-based stabilisation), VR, OSS (brand names)

**Servo AF**:
Continuous autofocus that keeps adjusting while the shutter button is half-pressed, for moving subjects. The opposite is One-Shot AF, which focuses once and locks.
_Avoid_: AI Servo (Canon's older name), AF-C (other brands)

**Subject detection**:
Autofocus that recognises a subject type (people, certain animals, vehicles), finds its face or eye, and tracks it. It covers only the subjects the camera's manual lists.
_Avoid_: eye-AF (that's one part of it), smart AF

## Files

**RAW**:
A file holding the sensor data with the camera's settings recorded but not applied, so white balance and tone can be redone in editing.
_Avoid_: digital negative, raw format (lower-case is fine in running text)
