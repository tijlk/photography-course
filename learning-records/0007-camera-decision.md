# Camera decided: Canon EOS R6 Mark II (used, dealer warranty) + RF 24-105 f/4L + RF 100-400

After a structured grilling session (2026-11-26) the learner chose a **Canon EOS R6 Mark II** (bought used from a large photo dealer, with 1-year warranty), the **RF 24-105mm f/4L IS USM** and the **RF 100-400mm f/5.6-8 IS USM**. The EF-S 17-55 will be sold (its condition is uncertain, and EF-S crops on full frame).

**Reasoning the learner endorsed**
- Full frame for low light in general (not just stars), easier background blur, and wider landscape options. These benefits continue after the trip.
- Invest in lenses, because bodies get replaced sooner. Don't go down in lens quality: f/2.8 on APS-C ≈ f/4.5 on full frame in depth of field, so a constant f/4 zoom is at least equivalent.
- 400mm reach is enough. The non-L 100-400 is the accepted exception (fits the two-lens hike, and is the biggest gain over the phone; 0.41× magnification as a taste of macro).
- Dual card slots don't matter (several unwiped cards instead). The R6 Mark II has them anyway.
- The R8 Mark II was the runner-up: lighter and new, but no fully mechanical shutter (6 fps distortion-free), a smaller viewfinder and ~220 vs ~450 shots per charge. A used R6 Mark II from a dealer cost less than a new R8 Mark II.

**Accessories planned**: one spare LP-E6NH battery, 4–5 SanDisk Extreme PRO UHS-I cards (UHS-II not needed), a 77 mm circular polarizer + 67→77 step-up ring, the ET-74B hood if it's not included, and optional good protection filters.
**Later**: RF 16mm f/2.8 + mini tripod (only for stars), a bag once the camera arrives, and a true macro lens after the trip.

**Implications**: Phase 3 is done. Phase 4 lessons target the R6 Mark II (Canon manual code C012 on cam.start.canon). The setup lesson must include exposure simulation ON (LR-0003), mention that no AF calibration is needed (LR-0005), cover lens + body stabilisation with the 100-400, and recap metering, handheld speed and white balance (spaced review). Also give a used-body check on arrival: sensor dust test at f/16 on a plain sky, all buttons, firmware.
