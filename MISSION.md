# Mission: Photography, back after a break since 2013

## Why
Come home with great photos from a family camper trip through the American Rocky Mountains in late July–August 2027, then grow photography into a long-term hobby from there.

## Success looks like
- Can choose, buy and confidently operate a modern camera (with or without the existing Canon EF-S 17-55mm) well before the trip.
- Gets sharp, well-exposed landscapes, wildlife, family/people and night-sky photos on the trip, without fiddling.
- Knows when to reach for the phone and when for the camera.
- Can send a few JPEGs from camera to phone on the road.
- Can edit the best shots afterwards in the free Lightroom mobile app, upgrading only if its limits actually bite.

## Constraints
- Background: ~10 years (2003–2013) on a Canon EOS 30D + EF-S 17-55mm; still owns the 17-55, no camera yet.
- Sequence: (1) basics self-check, (2) camera tech changes since 2013 (mirrorless, EVF, subject-detection AF, IBIS, high ISO), (3) buy a camera, (4) camera-specific lessons, (5) editing + subject lessons.
- Rhythm: 1–2 short sessions per week.
- On the trip: no editing and no backups; several memory cards, never wiped during the trip. A travel tripod is possible, not certain.
- Editing: free Lightroom mobile first; subscription only if needed.

## Out of scope
- Video.
- Communities (user opted out).
- Style trends beyond a light touch.
