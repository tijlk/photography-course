Photo workflow

Phone and camera photos in one Library, flowing one way only.

one-way flow temporary, during the trip

The rules

  1. The Library is home. Every finished photo ends up in Google Photos, where people are found and albums are made. Backup quality stays on Original quality ("back up with no change to quality"), not Storage saver, which compresses photos to 16 MP.
  2. Phone photos never leave the Library. They are edited only in Google Photos' own editor. The Darkroom is for camera RAW files only.
  3. The Archive holds camera Originals only (RAW + in-camera JPEG), one folder per trip or date. The Darkroom reads only from there and is never connected to Google Photos.
  4. Edits flow one way: Darkroom → Outbox → Library. Upload only the picks, then empty the Outbox. An Edit never goes back into the Archive or the Darkroom.
  5. Trip JPEGs are temporary: camera JPEGs sent to the phone also get backed up to the Library, so after the trip the same moment would appear twice (JPEG + Edit). Each evening, add the JPEGs you sent to the album "Trip – temporary". After the trip, once the Edits are uploaded, open that album, select all and delete. One manual step. Check on the first evening whether the received JPEGs keep the camera's capture time, and whether deleting in Google Photos also deletes from the iPhone.
  6. One clock: set the camera's date, time and time zone to match your phone before each trip, and when you change time zone. Exports keep their capture time.

A tip from a working photographer: decide per trip or event whether it's mainly a camera or mainly a phone occasion. Editing in Google Photos takes seconds; in the Darkroom, minutes per photo.

Check once

After your first Edit, check that Google Photos puts it at the right moment in your timeline. Whether Lightroom or darktable keep the capture time on export couldn't be confirmed from their documentation in this course. Also check that face recognition works for your account (Google Photos → Search → a row of faces; Wikipedia notes it is only available in some countries), and that an uploaded Edit keeps its full resolution.

Original, Archive, Darkroom, Edit, Outbox and Library are defined in the course glossary (GLOSSARY.md).
